# Lunch Break

There are plenty of great places to eat and relax in Winterthur:

* **Lunch in the Old Town:** You'll find numerous restaurants just a short walk away.
* **On Campus:** The [School of Engineering’s canteen](https://www.zfv.ch/de/essen-gehen/zhaw-cafeteria-mensa-eulachpassage#contact) is located in the TN building and offers a convenient lunch option.
* **Evening Chill:** For a cosy atmosphere later in the day, head over to Steinberggasse.

### Nearby Food Options

* **[Hasan's Sandwich](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6SXawiT13M4Eows47)**  
  Offers warm, customizable sandwiches, with vegetarian options available. Please note that seating is limited, making it ideal for takeaway. Prices are approximately CHF 12 per sandwich, and it is reported to be cash-only.

* **[Jimmy's Kebab & Co.](https://maps.app.goo.gl/owfQNyDbxH2PtiqGA)**  
  A local kebab shop offering a variety of traditional kebab options.

* **[Taman](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1d1peKgo11rgFewM9)**  
  A family-run kebab restaurant particularly well-known for its kebab plates (operated by the same family as Hasan's Sandwich).

* **[Burger Chuchi](https://maps.app.goo.gl/uyHZhT85r4NiQEKS8)**  
  Features customizable burgers. Combo meals including fries and a drink are available for approximately CHF 20.
